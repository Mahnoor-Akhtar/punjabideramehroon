Master Punjabi Ustaad

Heritage in Every Clay Pot

With over two decades of traditional Punjabi culinary heritage, our master chefs bring authentic dhaba and royal court recipes to life. Each dish is cooked in authentic clay tandoors using hand-ground spices and fresh white butter.

From the wheat fields of Punjab to Kharian's most beloved dining destination, Punjabi Dera is dedicated to preserving true Punjabi hospitality and rich timeless flavors.
